Winnie The Cat Support

Winnie The Cat is a simple app for saving cats you meet, adding notes, and feeding them virtually.

Common Questions

Does the app use GPS location?
No. Places are typed manually.

Where are my cat photos stored?
Your cat album is stored locally on your device.

Can I share a cat?
Yes. The share button opens the iOS share sheet.
