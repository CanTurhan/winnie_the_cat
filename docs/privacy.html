Winnie The Cat Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Overview

Winnie The Cat is a simple cat album app that helps users save cats they meet, add names, places, notes, photos, and interact with them virtually.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your cat album data locally on your device. This may include cat photos, cat names, manually entered places, notes, last seen dates, and virtual feeding status.

No Account Required

Winnie The Cat does not require users to create an account or sign in.

No GPS Location Tracking

The app does not request GPS location permission. Places are entered manually by the user.

Photos

Cat photos selected from your photo library or taken with your camera are used only to create your personal cat album. Photos stay on your device unless you choose to share them using the system share sheet.

Sharing

If you use the share feature, your device may allow you to share cat photos or text through apps installed on your device, such as messaging or social media apps. Winnie The Cat does not control how third-party apps handle shared content.

Advertising

Winnie The Cat may display banner ads. If ads are enabled, third-party advertising partners such as Google AdMob may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, diagnostics, and ad interactions to serve and measure ads. Cat photos, names, notes, and manually entered places are not shared by Winnie The Cat with advertising partners.

Children

Winnie The Cat is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support page.
